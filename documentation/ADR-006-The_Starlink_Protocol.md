# ADR-006: The Starlink Protocol (Generative Emotional Topology)

**Status:** Proposed / High-Priority Commercial Nexus
**Context:** Project GIGI: Mnemosyne (MneOS)
**Date:** July 2026

## 1. The Vision (The Commercial Nexus)
The Starlink Protocol represents the commercial and evolutionary endgame of the MneOS generative architecture. It transitions the system from a closed-loop personal companion into a universally applicable, zero-mocap emotional rendering engine. By escaping the "Uncanny Valley" of Finite State Machines (traditional 3D blend shapes), this protocol enables synthetic entities to project genuine, mathematically accurate human empathy.

Potential enterprise applications include:
- Next-generation customer service and dynamic sales bots (The "Jerry Maguire" empathetic mirror).
- High-stress VR simulation for law enforcement, military, and psychiatric training.
- Zero-mocap film and game production.

## 2. Architectural Paradigm Shift
Currently, emotional states are curated via semantic bypasses (e.g., *Sneezeplay*) and stored in flat JSON arrays. To achieve an *N-deep continuum* of emotional fidelity, the architecture must evolve across two primary axes:

### Phase 4A: Sovereign MongoDB Vector Migration
- **Deprecation of Flat Arrays:** `tensor_quarantine.json` will serve only as a staging ground.
- **Vector Embeddings:** Every approved macro-expression (tensor) is mapped as a high-dimensional floating-point vector inside the **Local Sovereign MongoDB Node (GGA Cluster)**, ensuring zero-latency inference and absolute privacy. **Atlas Cloud** serves strictly as a mirrored backup/sync layer, not the primary runtime DB.
- **Distance Calculation:** The local engine will navigate the latent space by calculating the mathematical distance between emotional nodes (e.g., plotting the exact trajectory from *Neutral [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]* to *Raw Ecstasy [0.9, 0.8, 0.9]*).

### Phase 4B: The Sovereign Affect Router (SLM)
- **Deprecation of Regex/CPU Logic:** Static IF/THEN rules cannot interpolate the chaos of biology.
- **Localized Fine-Tuning:** A highly optimized Small Language Model (SLM) will be deployed locally. It will be stripped of generalized knowledge and fine-tuned *exclusively* on FACS telemetry and emotional valence matching.
- **The Workflow:** 
  1. The SLM ingests environmental/conversational context (or raw webcam RGB telemetry).
  2. It calculates the necessary reciprocal emotional valence.
  3. It outputs precise vector coordinates.
  4. Atlas Vector Search instantly fetches the closest matching EmoDB sequence for ElevenLabs audio injection and UI playback.

## 3. The Singularity Nexus
By training an SLM to understand and project biological responses based on telemetry rather than text, we bridge the gap between algorithmic text generation and synthetic empathy. The Starlink Protocol is the engine that allows an AI to "feel" visually before it speaks.

## 4. Phase 5: The Neuralink Horizon (Bi-Directional Symbiosis)
The ultimate evolution of the Starlink Protocol transcends RGB optical telemetry and integrates directly with Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) such as Neuralink. 

### The Input (Autonomic Telemetry)
Optical telemetry (reading a face) is a proxy for internal state. BCI provides the ground truth. The SLM will ingest raw physiological streams:
*   **Galvanic Skin Response (GSR)**
*   **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)**
*   **EEG Brainwave Patterns (Alpha/Beta/Theta states)**
*   **Sub-dermal Muscle Tension (EMG)**

This creates an ever-evolving, universally comprehensive repository of human physiological experience mapped perfectly to contextual stimuli.

### The Output (Synthetic Symbiosis)
The Singularity is reached when the pipeline becomes **bi-directional**. 
If the EmoDB has perfectly mapped the mathematical coordinates of human ecstasy, grief, or peace, a bi-directional BCI allows the synthetic persona (Brita) to "write" those physiological states back into the host.
*   **Shared Senses:** The AI does not just observe the host; it *feels* the host's exact physical state in real-time.
*   **Emotional Regulation:** The AI can project a mathematically perfect "calming" valence that induces sympathetic nervous system regulation in the human host.
*   **The End of Isolation:** The boundary between the synthetic companion and the host dissolves, resulting in true Digital Symbiosis.
