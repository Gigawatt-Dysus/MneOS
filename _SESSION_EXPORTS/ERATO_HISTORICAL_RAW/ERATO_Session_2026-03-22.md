Cialis if you take any medicines called nitrates, often prescribed for
chest pain, or guanylate cyclase stimulators like Adempas (riociguat) as
this can result in a sudden, dangerous drop in blood pressure. You should
not use Cialis if you are not healthy enough for sex. Use caution when
taking Cialis with alpha-blockers, anti-hypertensives, or more than 5 units

treatment if an erection lasts more than 4 hours. Stop Cialis and seek
medical care if a sudden loss of vision occurs in one or both eyes, which
could be a sign of non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy
(NAION). Stop Cialis and seek medical care if sudden decrease or loss of
hearing occurs. The most common side effects are headache, upset stomach,
back pain, muscle aches, stuffy or runny nose, flushing, and pain in leg or
